Higher Studies

Structured mentorship and global admission pathways beyond undergraduate coursework: MS and PhD guidance, GRE/TOEFL preparation roadmaps, Statement of Purpose (SOP) reviews, and direct alumni and faculty conversations. The Higher Studies desk helps students plan their academic trajectory early—identifying target research groups, building publication-backed profiles, and navigating fellowships in India and abroad.

Whether you are targeting direct PhD tracks, thesis-based Master's programs, or interdisciplinary graduate fellowships, our working group connects your undergraduate lab work directly to faculty recommendation letters and graduate admission chairs.
